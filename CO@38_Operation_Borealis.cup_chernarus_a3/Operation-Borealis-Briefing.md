## Background

In March 2016, national elections were held across Chernarus. The political landscape had become increasingly divided between two major factions: the Democratic Party of Chernarus and the Red Movement of Chernarus.

Tensions erupted following allegations of corruption involving several high-ranking government officials. Public protests quickly spread throughout the country, drawing thousands of demonstrators. As unrest intensified, Military Police units were deployed to restore order. What began as civil demonstrations soon descended into violent clashes between protesters and counter-protesters, with widespread rioting, arson, and street fighting.

The situation reached a breaking point when government security forces opened fire on demonstrators. The incident ignited nationwide outrage and deepened the divide within the population.

<img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1511256454030557345/1516153166411071649/20260615205105_1.jpg?ex=6a319b2b&is=6a3049ab&hm=d574f0a6767b6a9e575ddff3b6aca513b290bc4d94c029ef23bd1a5da1608e6c" alt="image" width="1200" />

In the chaos that followed, a coalition of senior military officers launched a coup in support of the communist-aligned Chedaki movement. Although government leaders successfully escaped into exile in a neighboring allied nation, the Chernarussian Defence Force (CDF) was forced into a fighting retreat as Chedaki forces rapidly seized territory across the country.

Pushed back to the northern regions still loyal to the legitimate government, the CDF regrouped and prepared a counteroffensive with support from NATO and regional allies.

Meanwhile, resistance movements emerged behind enemy lines. The National Party of Chernarus (NAPA) organized into decentralized cells, conducting raids against Chedaki supply depots, ambushing convoys, and relaying critical intelligence to CDF command.

##  Situation Report  
**April 2016**

Months of fighting have begun to take their toll on the Chedaki war effort. Overextended supply lines, growing partisan activity, and an effective CDF defense-in-depth strategy have weakened enemy frontline positions.

The newly formed **1st Elite Hussar Regiment** has been equipped with NATO-supplied vehicles, including M125 MRAPs, while the CDF Air Force now fields upgraded strike capabilities with laser-guided munitions. Rotary-wing support includes UH-60 Blackhawks, Ghosthawks, and MH-6 Little Birds.

A breakthrough has finally been achieved along the frontline.

The objective is clear: exploit the gap, link up with surrounded NAPA resistance fighters at **Outpost Harvest**, and establish **Task Force Alpha** for offensive operations deep behind enemy lines.

NAPA cells operating around **Vyshnoye**, **Mogilevka**, and **Nadezhdino** have severely disrupted enemy logistics. Recognizing the threat, Chedaki commanders have begun concentrating militia forces to eliminate the resistance stronghold at Outpost Harvest.

The 1st Elite Hussar Regiment must reach the outpost before it is overrun.

Following the link-up, Task Force Alpha will launch coordinated assaults against **Nadezhdino** and **Mogilevka**, cutting off reinforcement routes from **Chernogorsk**, where newly raised militia forces are assembling with assistance from a rogue foreign power.

#   Briefing – Guerrillas of Liberation (GOL)  
## Mission
**1st Platoon "Task Force Alpha"**, attached to the 1st Elite Hussar Regiment, will advance through friendly lines and exploit the breach in the enemy front.

Your objective is to break through Chedaki defenses and establish contact with NAPA resistance fighters positioned in the hills south of Vyshnoye.

Enemy reserve formations are moving north to seal the breach and stabilize the frontline. These forces must be bypassed, defeated, or fixed in place while the platoon continues its advance.

The relief of Outpost Harvest is the highest priority.

#  Commander's Intent  
The success of Operation Borealis hinges on speed, aggression, and coordination.

1st Platoon must break through enemy lines and relieve the defenders of Outpost Harvest before they are overwhelmed. Once united, Task Force Alpha will strike deep into enemy-controlled territory, seize key settlements, and sever the flow of reinforcements from Chernogorsk.

Failure will allow the enemy to regroup and reinforce the front.

Success will mark the beginning of the CDF counteroffensive to reclaim Chernarus.
